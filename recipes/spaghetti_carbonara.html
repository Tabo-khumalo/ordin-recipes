Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

Spaghetti Carbonara is a delicious Italian classic that combines perfectly cooked spaghetti with a rich, silky sauce made from eggs, Parmesan cheese and freshly cracked black pepper. Crispy bacon adds a savoury, smoky flavour, while the cheese melts into the pasta to create a creamy texture without the need for cream.

Finished with extra grated Parmesan and a generous sprinkle of black pepper, this simple yet satisfying dish is perfect for a quick weeknight dinner or a comforting meal to share.

Ingredients

  • 250 g spaghetti
  • 150 g bacon or pancetta, diced
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese (or Pecorino Romano)
  • 2 cloves garlic (optional)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil (optional)

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the spaghetti and cook according to the package instructions until al dente. Before draining, reserve about 1 cup of pasta water.
  2. Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the diced bacon or pancetta and cook until crispy and golden. If using garlic, add it during the last minute of cooking for extra flavour.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, egg yolk, grated Parmesan cheese and a generous amount of freshly ground black pepper until well combined.
  4. Add the drained spaghetti to the pan with the bacon. Toss everything together, then remove the pan from the heat.
  5. Pour the egg and cheese mixture over the hot pasta while tossing continuously. Add a little reserved pasta water at a time to help create a smooth, silky sauce. The residual heat will gently cook the eggs without scrambling them.
  6. Plate the spaghetti immediately. Finish with extra grated Parmesan cheese and freshly ground black pepper.
